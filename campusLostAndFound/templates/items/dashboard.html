How It Works

1. Report

Report a lost or found item with its name, category, photo, date, and campus location, such as a classroom, library, or cafeteria.

2. Smart Match

AI-assisted matching compares item descriptions to suggest possible matches between lost and found belongings.

3. Connect

Review potential matches and contact the person who posted the item to help reunite belongings with their rightful owners.
